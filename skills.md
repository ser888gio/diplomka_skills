# Diploma Thesis Guide – PEF ČZU Praha

## Mandatory Chapter Structure

The chapter order and numbering below is **binding** (DP = diplomová práce, BP = bakalářská práce):

1. **Úvod** – ¾ to 1 page. General introduction to the topic, no subchapters.
2. **Cíl práce a metodika** – ~1 page. Exact or refined version of the assignment. Never contradict the zadání. Write in present or past tense in the thesis itself; future tense only in the zadání.
3. **Teoretická část práce** (or *Teoretická východiska* / *Současný stav poznání*) – minimum ½ of total length. Literature review; critical comparison of sources. Every page must contain at least one citation. Recommended subchapters: *Obdobná řešení* (comparable solutions) and *Shrnutí* (summary linking theory to the practical part).
4. **Vlastní práce** (or *Analytická část* / *Praktická část* / *Empirická část*) – documents the procedure, data collection, and application of methods in enough detail that someone else could replicate it.
5. **Zhodnocení výsledků / Výsledky a diskuse / Zhodnocení a doporučení** – 2–4 pages. Results, their evaluation against the main goal, and comparison with other studies.
6. **Závěr** – 1–2 pages. Summary of all results, answers to all goals, contribution of the work, suggestions for further research.
7. **Seznam použitých zdrojů** – full bibliography.
8. **Seznam obrázků; Seznam tabulek; Seznam grafů; Seznam zkratek** – as applicable.
9. **Přílohy** – optional.

> Only one name variant per chapter may be chosen (marked with *).

## Scope and Formatting

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| DP length | 60–80 pages (Úvod through Závěr) |
| Theoretical part | Minimum ½ of total length |
| Font | Times New Roman 12 pt |
| Line spacing | 1.5 |
| Margins | Top 30 mm, inner 35 mm, bottom 25 mm, outer 20 mm |
| Paper | A4, double-sided printing recommended |

- Every main chapter starts on a new page.
- Subchapters go to a maximum of 3 levels in the table of contents (4 levels in the text).
- Every paragraph = one idea, minimum 3 lines. Every numbered chapter has at least 3 paragraphs.
- Write in the **3rd person passive voice**: *"Bylo zjištěno, že…"* — never use 1st person singular or plural.
- Use the official PEF template from **zp.pef.czu.cz**.

## Goals and Methodology

- The **main goal** must be achievable and verifiable. It is written in **present tense** in the thesis.
- Do not confuse goals with methods. A goal cannot be "to analyze", "to describe", or "to compare" — those are methods. A goal is to *create*, *identify*, *evaluate*, *determine*, *design*, etc.
- **Partial goals** (dílčí cíle) together lead to the main goal.
- The methodology describes which empirical methods are used: experiment, measurement, observation. Developing an application using a framework is **not** a methodology.
- The thesis must never contradict the approved zadání.

## Citation Rules (ČSN ISO 690:2011)

### When to Cite
- Every paragraph in the theoretical part should contain at least one citation.
- No page in the theoretical part should be without a citation.
- Every figure, table, and graph must cite its source directly below it.
- Direct quotes must be in **quotation marks and italics**.
- Prefer **paraphrase** (indirect citation) over direct quotes.

### Choosing a Citation Method

**Option - Harvard (name–date)**
Inline: `(Novák, 2020, s. 45)` or `Novák (2020, s. 45) uvádí, že…`
Bibliography: alphabetical by surname, year after name.

### Bibliography Format (ISO 690)

**Printed book:**
```
PŘÍJMENÍ, J. Rok. Název: podnázev. Vydání. Místo: Nakladatel. Stran s. ISBN.
```
Example:
```
JUREČKA, V. a kol. 2017. Makroekonomie. 3. vyd. Praha: Grada Publishing. 368 s. ISBN 978-80-271-0251-8.
```

**Journal article:**
```
PŘÍJMENÍ, J. Rok. Název článku. Název časopisu, ročník(číslo): strany. DOI nebo URL.
```

**Web page:**
```
AUTOR nebo ORGANIZACE. Název stránky [online]. Datum publikace [cit. RRRR-MM-DD]. Dostupné z: URL.
```

**Multiple authors:** list all (up to 4), or use `a kol.` for more.

### Common Citation Mistakes to Avoid
- Do **not** cite Wikipedia (unknown author, content can change anytime).
- Do **not** cite scripts (skripta) as primary sources.
- Sources should generally be **no older than 10 years**; always include at least one English-language source.
- Minimum **5 book sources** listed in the zadání; these should actually be used in the thesis.
- Use a citation manager (Citace PRO at citace.com — free with ČZU login, Mendeley, or Word's built-in tool).
- Foreign-language titles are **not translated** — keep them in the original script.

## Figures, Tables, and Graphs

Every visual element must have:
1. **Caption** with sequential number and descriptive title (e.g., *Obrázek 1 Název*, *Tabulka 3 Název*) — numbered separately for figures, tables, and graphs across the whole thesis.
2. **Source** cited directly below: `Zdroj: Novák (2020)` or `Zdroj: vlastní zpracování`.
3. **In-text reference** before the object appears: *"…viz tabulka 3"*.

The caption, object, and source must always appear on the **same page**. All objects are centered. Caption font: Times New Roman 12 pt (same as body text).

Before reproducing any figure or table from another source, verify the license allows it; otherwise obtain written permission from the author.

## Front Matter (Vstupní část)

In order:
1. Title page (*titulní list*) — school, faculty, department, thesis type, title, author (no titles), © year ČZU v Praze.
2. *Zadání práce* — inserted from UIS (printed and electronic versions).
3. *Čestné prohlášení* — declaration of independent work, date, signature.
4. *Poděkování* — optional.
5. First numbered page — Czech and English title, **abstract** (max. 15 lines), **keywords** (7–10 terms), English abstract and keywords.
6. *Obsah* — table of contents, headings up to level 3 only.

## Language and Style

- Use formal academic language; avoid colloquial or journalistic style.
- Write in the **3rd person passive**: *"Bylo ověřeno…"*, *"Je použito…"*
- You are **not** writing a manual, textbook, or tutorial.
- Each paragraph covers one idea (minimum 3 lines).
- Use non-breaking spaces (Ctrl+Shift+Space) to prevent orphaned prepositions at line ends.
- Use Word **styles** for headings so numbering, table of contents, and lists of figures/tables generate automatically.
- Have the thesis proofread by someone else before submission.

## Plagiarism Limits (Theses.cz Check)

| Situation | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Uncited text matching other authors | > 10 % → plagiarism |
| Cited text matching other authors | > 20 % → plagiarism (compilat) |
| Match with own previously published work | ≤ 40 % allowed |
| Supervisor must comment in review | ≥ 15 % match |
